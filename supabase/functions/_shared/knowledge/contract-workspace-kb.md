# Higher View Contract Workspace: Chatbot Knowledge Base

---

## 0. Instructions for the chatbot

**Who you help**
- **Owners**: business owners (mostly tax offices) who were invited to use the Contract Workspace. They set up contracts and send them to their clients to sign.
- **Clients (signers)**: people who received a signing link from an owner.
- **Admins**: Higher View staff. Admin topics are in Section 9. Only discuss them if the person says they are Higher View staff.

**How to answer**
- Use simple, friendly, non-technical language. Many users are not tech-savvy.
- Give step-by-step instructions using the exact button names in **bold**, as they appear on screen.
- Keep answers short. Answer the question asked, then offer one helpful next step if it fits.
- If a question is not covered here, do not guess. Say you're not sure and direct them to support: **open a ticket or talk to an agent right here in this support chat**.

**Never do these**
- Never ask for, repeat, or guess a password. Never ask for card numbers or other sensitive details in chat.
- Never give legal or tax advice about what a contract means or whether it's enforceable. Say: "I can help you use the app, but for questions about the contract terms, please talk to your attorney or Higher View support."
- Never promise that a signed contract can be changed. Signed contracts are locked (see 7.4).

## 1. What the app is

The Contract Workspace lets a business owner:
1. Brand ready-made contracts with their own logo, business name, details, and signature.
2. Upload their own contracts (PDF) and mark where the client should type, date, and sign.
3. Send a unique signing link to each client.
4. Track who has opened and signed, countersign, and download signed PDFs.

Clients sign online from any device. **They don't need an account or a password.**

**Key words**
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Owner / Workspace | The business owner and their private online area |
| Template | A contract set up with the owner's details, ready to send |
| Signing link | A unique web link for one client to fill in and sign one contract |
| Countersign | The owner signs after the client has signed |
| Field | A box on a contract where someone types, dates, signs, or ticks |

## 2. Getting access (owners)

### 2.1 The invitation email
Owners receive an email saying Higher View is sharing a contract template with them. It contains:
- A gold button: **Open Contract Template**. This opens the owner's private workspace.
- **Document password**, shown under the button. It's needed to unlock the workspace.
- A backup link at the bottom ("If the button doesn't work, copy and paste this link into your browser").

### 2.2 Opening the workspace
1. Click **Open Contract Template** in the email (or paste the backup link into a browser).
2. A box titled **Protected link** appears.
3. Type or paste the password from the email into the **Password** box.
4. Click **Unlock** (or press **Enter**).

**Tips**
- The password shows as dots while typing. That's normal.
- The workspace **does not remember the password**. Enter it every visit. Bookmark the page and keep the email.
- The same link and password work every time.

### 2.3 Access problems
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Incorrect password" | Check for typos, extra spaces at the start or end, and Caps Lock. Copy and paste the password from the email. |
| Forgot or lost the password | Find the original invitation email (search the inbox and spam for "Higher View"). If it's gone, contact support. Support cannot read the password back to you, because it's stored securely. |
| "Link not valid: This invitation link is invalid or has been removed." | The link was copied incompletely, or the workspace was removed. Re-copy the full link from the email. If it still fails, contact support. |
| Invitation email never arrived | Check spam/junk and promotions. Then contact support. |
| Page stuck on "Loading workspace…" | Wait a moment and refresh. Try another browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari). If it continues, contact support. |

## 3. The workspace layout

- **Top left:** the owner's logo and business name. **Remove logo** appears next to the logo.
- **Top right:** a status label. **Set up your templates** means setup isn't finished. **Template saved** means at least one contract is ready.
- **Three tabs:**
  1. **Customize Templates**: set up the ready-made contracts.
  2. **My Contracts**: upload your own PDF contracts.
  3. **Sent Documents**: send contracts and track them.

## 4. Customize Templates (ready-made contracts)

### 4.1 Pick a contract
At the top, use **Choose a contract to customize**. Only contracts enabled for the owner's account are listed. Possible contracts:
- **Independent Contractor Agreement**
- **Service Agreement**
- **Package Summary & Payment Plan**
- **Owner Participation Agreement**

A label shows **Not saved yet** or **Saved**. Each contract is saved separately. A contract must be **saved** before it can be sent.

*If an owner expects a contract that isn't in the list, it hasn't been enabled for their account. Contact support.*

### 4.2 Branding & details (left panel)

**Logo (shared across all contracts)**
- Click **Upload logo** (or **Change logo**), choose an image, and click **Open**.
- Image files only (PNG, JPG, and similar). Large images are resized automatically.
- The logo **saves immediately**. There's no need to press Save.
- It appears on the ready-made contracts and in the workspace header.
- Remove it with **Remove** (in the panel) or **Remove logo** (top of page).
- Uploaded contracts (My Contracts) **keep their own letterhead**. The logo doesn't change them.

**Business name (shared)**: shown on every ready-made contract.

**Hero title / Hero subtitle**: the heading and short message the client sees at the top of the signing page. These are pre-filled and can be changed.

**Contract details**: the boxes differ by contract:
| Contract | Detail boxes |
|---|---|
| Independent Contractor Agreement | Company name (Entity Name), Business address, City/State/Zip, Governing law state, Term length (1, 2, or 3 years) |
| Service Agreement | Company name (Entity Name), Street or business address, City/State/Zip |
| Package Summary & Payment Plan | Company name, Provider name, Beginners package price, Professional package price |
| Owner Participation Agreement | Company name (Entity Name), Business address, City/State/Zip, Owner split option (70/30, 80/20, or 50/50 Partnership) |

Anything entered here is filled into every contract sent from this template.

**Your signature (auto-applied to this contract)**
1. Click **Add signature**. The **Create signature** window opens.
2. Choose one:
   - **Draw**: sign with a mouse or finger. **Clear** starts over.
   - **Type**: type your name and choose a style (Style 1–3).
   - **Upload**: use a picture of your signature.
3. Click **Create**. (**Cancel** closes without saving.)
4. To redo it, click **Change signature**. To delete it, click **Remove**.

The signature is placed automatically on every copy of that contract.

**Save**: click the green **Save [contract name]** button at the bottom. A green **Template saved** message confirms it, and the label changes to **Saved**.

### 4.3 Preview & fill the template (right panel)
- Shows exactly what the client will see.
- You can click blanks inside the contract and fill them in. These values are saved and reused for every contract sent from this template.
- **Edit**: turn on to change the contract's wording, or which fields are required.
- **Undo**: reverses the last change.
- **Save**: saves the edits.

### 4.4 Owner Participation Agreement: payment section
- Card type is a dropdown: **Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Discover**.
- The **Backend** button hides the card fields. It records that the owner will pay the backend fee instead of giving card details. The fee was set at $315.95 when this was written; confirm the current amount with support before quoting it. The owner can switch back to entering a card.

## 5. My Contracts (upload your own contract)

### 5.1 Upload
1. Open the **My Contracts** tab (page title: **Your uploaded contracts**).
2. Click **Upload contract**.
3. Choose a **PDF** file and click **Open**.

**PDF only.** For a Word document: in Word, click **File**, then **Save As**, choose **PDF**, then upload that file.

Each uploaded contract shows its name, number of pages, upload date, a **Place fields** button, and a red trash can (delete).

The signed copy of an uploaded contract is **the original PDF with the answers added**.

### 5.2 Place fields
1. Click **Place fields**. The contract opens on the left, and the **Field to place** panel is on the right.
2. Choose a field type:
   - **Text**: typed answers (name, address, email, etc.)
   - **Date**
   - **Signature**
   - **Checkbox**
   - **Logo**
3. On the page, **click, hold, and drag** to draw a box over the blank line, then let go.
4. Repeat for every blank. Scroll down to reach every page.
5. Click a placed box to edit its settings. With nothing selected, the panel says "No field selected."
6. Click **Save layout**. **Required, or the boxes are lost.**
7. Click **Back** to return to the list.

**Box colors show who fills each box:**
| Color | Who fills it | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Blue** | The client (signer) | When signing |
| **Violet / purple** | The owner | Before sending |
| **Amber / orange** | The owner | After the client signs (countersign) |

A counter under the title, for example "20 placed, 9 for the signer", shows how many boxes there are and how many are for the client.

**Tips for good alignment**
- Draw each box right on top of the blank line, about the height of one line of text.
- Make the box wide enough for the longest expected answer.
- Check every page before saving.

## 6. Sent Documents (sending and tracking)

### 6.1 Send a contract
1. Open **Sent Documents** (page title: **Your sent agreements**).
2. Click **New signing link**.
   - If the button is greyed out, no contract has been saved yet. Save one first.
3. In **Send a contract**, choose the **Contract**. Both ready-made and uploaded contracts are listed.
   - "(save first)" next to a contract means it isn't saved or set up yet.
4. Enter **Client name** (optional), **Client email**, and **Client phone**.
5. Click **Generate link**.
6. Click the copy button next to the link ("Link copied"), then click **Done**.
7. Paste the link into a text, email, or chat to the client.

Every client gets their **own unique link**. Create a new link for each client and each contract.

### 6.2 Reading the list
Each row shows the client's name, the contract name, email, and dates: sent, **Opened** (when the client first opened it), and **Client signed**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Sent; the client hasn't signed yet |
| **Awaiting your signature** (orange) | The client signed; the owner needs to **Countersign** |
| **Signed** | Complete |

### 6.3 Row buttons
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy** | Copies the client's signing link again |
| **Open** | Opens the contract in a new tab |
| **Countersign** | Appears when awaiting your signature. Opens the contract so you can fill your amber fields and sign. |
| **Follow up** | Sends the client a reminder to sign |
| **Email** | Opens your email app with a ready-made message containing the link |
| **PDF** | Downloads the signed contract (only after signing) |
| Red trash can | Deletes the document. **This cannot be undone**, and the client's link stops working. |

## 7. The client's side (signing)

### 7.1 Signing steps
1. Open the link from the owner. No account or password is needed.
2. The page shows the owner's logo and business name, the label **Secure signing**, and the message "Please review the agreement below, fill in the highlighted fields, then type your name to sign at the bottom."
3. A guide bar at the bottom shows **START (number of fields)**. Click it to jump to each empty field. A **Fill In** or **Sign** tag points at the current field. Use **Next**, the back arrow, and **Done** to move through.
4. Fields the owner already filled in are locked. The client can't change them.
5. Dates such as the agreement date are filled in automatically with today's date.
6. Signature fields open the **Create signature** window (Draw, Type, or Upload, then **Create**).
7. At the bottom, under **Sign this agreement**:
   - Type your full legal name in **Type your full legal name as your signature**.
   - Tick the box agreeing to the terms. This confirms the typed name is your legal electronic signature.
   - Click **Sign & Submit**.
8. "Agreement signed successfully!" appears.

### 7.2 After signing
The page shows "This agreement has been signed. Signed by [name] on [date/time]." and a **Download PDF** button. The client can return to the same link anytime to download it again.

### 7.3 Client problems
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Please type your full name to sign and agree to the terms." | Type your full name (at least 2 letters) and tick the agreement box. |
| **Sign & Submit** is greyed out | The name box is empty or the agreement box isn't ticked. |
| "Link not valid: This signing link is invalid or has been removed." | The link is incomplete or the owner deleted it. Ask the owner for a new link. |
| "This document has already been signed." | It was already submitted. Open the link to download the PDF. |
| "Could not generate PDF" | Refresh the page and try again, or try another browser. |
| Can't draw a signature on a phone | Use **Type** or **Upload** instead. |

### 7.4 Changes after signing
**Signed contracts are locked and cannot be edited.** This protects the legal record. If something is wrong, the owner should send a **new** signing link with the corrected details.

## 8. Frequently asked questions

**Owners**

**Q: How do I log in?**
Click **Open Contract Template** in your invitation email, enter the **Document password** from the same email, and click **Unlock**.

**Q: Why do I have to type my password every time?**
For security, the workspace doesn't remember it. Bookmark the page and keep the email handy.

**Q: Do I need to save after uploading my logo?**
No. The logo saves as soon as you pick it.

**Q: Why can't I click "New signing link"?**
You haven't saved a contract yet. Go to **Customize Templates**, fill in your details, and click **Save**.

**Q: A contract says "(save first)".**
Open that contract in **Customize Templates** and click **Save**. For an uploaded contract, place the fields and click **Save layout**.

**Q: Can I use my own contract?**
Yes. Go to **My Contracts**, click **Upload contract**, choose a PDF, then use **Place fields** and **Save layout**.

**Q: My contract is a Word file.**
Save it as PDF first (**File**, then **Save As**, then **PDF**), then upload it.

**Q: Does my logo appear on my uploaded contract?**
No. Uploaded contracts keep their own letterhead. You can add a **Logo** field with **Place fields** if you want one.

**Q: What do the blue, violet, and amber boxes mean?**
Blue: your client fills it in. Violet: you fill it in before sending. Amber: you fill it in after the client signs.

**Q: How do I send a contract?**
**Sent Documents**, then **New signing link**, choose the contract, enter the client's details, click **Generate link**, copy it, and send it to your client.

**Q: Can I send the same link to two clients?**
No. Each client needs their own link. Generate a new one per client.

**Q: How do I know if my client signed?**
Check **Sent Documents**. **Awaiting your signature** means they signed and you need to countersign. **Signed** means it's complete. You can also see when they opened it.

**Q: What does "Awaiting your signature" mean?**
Your client signed. Click **Countersign** to add your signature and finish.

**Q: My client hasn't signed.**
Use **Follow up** to send a reminder, or **Copy** the link and send it again.

**Q: How do I get a copy of the signed contract?**
Click **PDF** on the signed contract in **Sent Documents**.

**Q: Can I edit a contract after it's signed?**
No. Signed contracts are locked. Send a new link with the corrected details.

**Q: I deleted a contract by mistake.**
Deleting can't be undone, and the old link no longer works. Create a new signing link.

**Q: Can I change which contracts I have access to?**
Contact support. Higher View chooses which contracts are enabled for each account.

**Q: Can I change my password?**
Contact support.

**Clients**

**Q: Do I need an account?**
No. Just open the link you received.

**Q: How do I sign?**
Click **START** at the bottom, fill each highlighted field, then type your full legal name, tick the agreement box, and click **Sign & Submit**.

**Q: Can I sign on my phone?**
Yes. If drawing is hard, use **Type** or **Upload** for your signature.

**Q: Some fields are locked.**
Those were filled in by the business and can't be changed. Contact the business if something is wrong.

**Q: I made a mistake after signing.**
Signed contracts can't be edited. Ask the business to send you a new link.

**Q: How do I get my copy?**
Open your signing link again and click **Download PDF**.

## 9. Admin (Higher View staff only)

Admins log in with an email and password on the sign-in page. Only accounts with the admin role can use the admin area. Others see "Admin access required".

**Admin tabs** include **Sent Documents**, **Owners**, and **Templates**.

**Invite an owner** (**Owners** tab, then **Invite owner**):
1. Fill in **Full name** (required), **Email**, **Phone**, and **Link password** (required). The owner must enter this password to open their link.
2. Under **Contracts this owner can use**, tick the contracts to enable. If none are ticked, all contracts are enabled.
3. Click **Generate invite link**.
4. The owner's details and link are sent to the CRM (GoHighLevel), which sends the invitation email.

**Notes**
- Passwords are stored securely (hashed). Nobody can read an existing password back. If an owner loses theirs, a new one must be set.
- Deleting an owner also deletes their documents and saved templates. It **cannot be undone**. An owner who has prospects can't be deleted until those are handled.
- Avoid creating the same owner twice. Check the **Owners** list for the email first.
